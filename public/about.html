Who We Are

Making technology easier to manage.

Technology is most useful when people can depend on it. When technical problems occur, a clear process for reporting issues and following up can make support easier to coordinate.

The Costech Help Desk project applies that principle to IT service requests. It brings ticket submission, request tracking, administrative oversight, and staff assignment into one organised workflow.

The goal is to make support requests easier to record, monitor, and manage without relying entirely on scattered messages or informal follow-ups.

01

Structured Requests

Users can describe their technical problems and submit requests through a dedicated portal.

02

Clearer Tracking

Ticket information and status updates help users and support staff follow requests more easily.

03

Centralised Management

Administrative tools help organise tickets, assignments, staff information, and reports.

The Help Desk

One system. A clearer workflow.

The Costech Help Desk is designed to organise the everyday stages of IT support, from reporting an issue to managing its progress.

01

For Clients

Register or sign in, submit a ticket, describe the issue, select its category and priority, and review submitted requests from the client dashboard.

02

For Administrators

Review tickets, search and filter requests, update ticket information, assign support staff, manage client and staff records, and view reports.

03

For Service Management

Keep ticket status, resolution notes, and relevant service and payment information together to support more organised record-keeping.

Our Approach
