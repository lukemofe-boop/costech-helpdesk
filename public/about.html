THE IT SUPPORT SYSTEM

A simpler way to manage technical support.

The Costech Help Desk Management System provides a centralized platform for managing IT support requests.

Clients can create accounts, submit technical support requests and track the progress of their requests.

Support administrators can monitor requests, assign support staff, update request statuses, record resolution notes and manage registered clients.
